# Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Template

A Quarterly Business Review (QBR) is an executive communication framework used by Product Managers and Product Leaders to review performance, explain decisions, and align on future priorities.

A strong QBR connects:

```
Product Performance

↓

Business Outcomes

↓

Strategic Decisions

↓

Future Investments
```

---

# QBR Structure

```
Executive Summary

↓

Business Performance

↓

Product Performance

↓

Customer Insights

↓

Financial Impact

↓

Strategic Priorities

↓

Next Quarter Plan
```

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Keep this section concise.

Include:

- Biggest achievements
- Major challenges
- Business impact
- Key decisions needed

Example:

```
Q2 product adoption increased 35%.
Enterprise expansion drove $2M additional ARR.
Main focus next quarter is improving retention.
```

---

# 2. Business Overview

Explain overall business context.

Include:

- Market changes
- Company priorities
- Strategic initiatives

Questions:

- What changed this quarter?
- What matters most for the business?

---

# 3. Product Performance

Review product health.

---

## Adoption Metrics

Track:

- Active users
- New users
- Feature adoption
- Activation rate

---

## Engagement Metrics

Track:

- Usage frequency
- Session activity
- Workflow completion

---

## Retention Metrics

Track:

- Customer retention
- User retention
- Churn
- Expansion

---

# 4. Financial Performance

Connect product metrics to financial outcomes.

---

## Revenue

Track:

- ARR
- MRR
- Revenue growth
- Expansion revenue

---

## Monetization

Track:

- ARPU
- Conversion rate
- Pricing impact
- Upsell

---

## Profitability

Track:

- Gross margin
- Contribution margin
- Cost efficiency

---

# 5. Customer Insights

Explain what customers are telling you.

---

## Customer Feedback

Include:

- Common requests
- Pain points
- Complaints
- Success stories

---

## Customer Segments

Analyze:

- Enterprise
- Mid-market
- SMB
- Free users

---

## Customer Health

Track:

- Satisfaction
- NPS
- Renewal risk

---

# 6. Product Delivery Review

Review execution.

---

## Completed Initiatives

Include:

- Major launches
- Improvements
- Experiments

---

## Impact Delivered

Explain:

Not only:

```
What was shipped?
```

But:

```
What changed because of it?
```

---

# 7. Experiment Results

For experiments:

Document:

## Hypothesis

Example:

```
Improving onboarding will increase activation.
```

---

## Result

Include:

- Metrics
- Learnings
- Decision

---

## Next Step

Choose:

- Scale
- Iterate
- Stop

---

# 8. Product Economics

Review product financial health.

---

## Revenue Drivers

Examples:

- New customers
- Expansion
- Pricing

---

## Cost Drivers

Examples:

- Infrastructure
- Support
- Engineering investment

---

## Efficiency Metrics

Examples:

- CAC
- LTV
- Payback period
- Gross margin

---

# 9. Strategic Learnings

Discuss:

## What Worked?

Examples:

- Successful launches
- Growth channels
- Customer segments

---

## What Did Not Work?

Examples:

- Failed experiments
- Adoption issues
- Market assumptions

---

## What Changed?

Examples:

- Customer behavior
- Competition
- Technology

---

# 10. Next Quarter Priorities

Define priorities clearly.

For each initiative include:

## Goal

What outcome are we trying to achieve?

---

## Investment

What resources are required?

---

## Expected Impact

How will success be measured?

---

## Metrics

Which KPIs will move?

---

# Priority Template

| Initiative | Goal | Investment | Expected Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feature A | Improve retention | 3 engineers | +5% retention |
| Product B | Increase revenue | Pricing work | +$500K ARR |

---

# 11. Risks & Dependencies

Document:

## Risks

Examples:

- Adoption uncertainty
- Technical complexity
- Market changes

---

## Dependencies

Examples:

- Engineering teams
- Data availability
- External partners

---

# 12. Executive Decisions Needed

End every QBR with clear asks.

Examples:

```
Decision Needed:

Approve investment in AI platform.

Reason:

Expected $3M annual impact.

Timeline:

Q3-Q4.
```
