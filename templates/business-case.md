# Business Case Template

A structured framework for Product Managers to evaluate, justify, and communicate new product initiatives.

A business case connects:

```
Customer Problem

↓

Product Solution

↓

Business Impact

↓

Investment Decision
```

---

# 1. Executive Summary

## Initiative Name

Example:

```
Self-Service Analytics Dashboard
```

---

## One-Line Summary

Describe the initiative in one sentence.

Example:

```
Enable enterprise customers to analyze performance independently and reduce support dependency.
```

---

## Recommendation

Example:

```
Invest in building the analytics platform because it can increase retention and create expansion revenue.
```

---

# 2. Problem Definition

## Customer Problem

Describe:

- Who experiences the problem?
- How frequently does it happen?
- What is the current workaround?

---

## Business Problem

Describe:

- Revenue impact
- Cost impact
- Strategic impact

Example:

```
High support dependency increases operational costs and limits enterprise scalability.
```

---

# 3. Market Opportunity

Analyze:

## Target Customers

Define:

- Segment
- Size
- Needs
- Buying behavior

---

## Market Potential

Evaluate:

- Market size
- Growth rate
- Competitive landscape

---

## Competitive Context

Analyze:

- Existing alternatives
- Competitor capabilities
- Market expectations

---

# 4. Proposed Solution

Describe:

## Product Approach

Include:

- Core capabilities
- User experience
- Technical requirements

---

## Scope

### Included

Examples:

- Dashboard
- Reporting
- Export functionality

---

### Not Included

Examples:

- Advanced forecasting
- Custom integrations

---

# 5. Customer Value

Explain why customers will care.

Measure:

## User Benefits

Examples:

- Saves time
- Reduces complexity
- Improves decisions

---

## Business Benefits

Examples:

- More revenue
- Lower costs
- Better retention

---

# 6. Success Metrics

Define measurable outcomes.

---

## Product Metrics

Examples:

- Adoption rate
- Activation
- Engagement
- Usage frequency

---

## Business Metrics

Examples:

- ARR impact
- Conversion
- Retention
- Cost savings

---

## Customer Metrics

Examples:

- Satisfaction
- NPS
- Support reduction

---

# 7. Investment Required

Estimate resources.

---

## Team Requirements

Include:

- Product
- Engineering
- Design
- Data
- Marketing

---

## Timeline

Example:

```
Discovery: 1 month

Build: 3 months

Launch: 1 month
```

---

## Cost Estimate

Include:

- People cost
- Infrastructure cost
- Operational cost

---

# 8. Financial Analysis

## Revenue Impact

Estimate:

```
Additional Customers

×

Revenue Per Customer
```

---

## Cost Savings

Estimate:

```
Current Cost

-

Future Cost
```

---

## ROI

Formula:

```
ROI

=

(Value Created - Investment)

/

Investment
```

---

## Payback Period

Formula:

```
Investment

/

Monthly Benefit
```

---

# 9. Alternatives Considered

Evaluate options.

---

## Alternative 1: Do Nothing

Impact:

- Lost opportunity
- Competitive risk

---

## Alternative 2: Build Smaller Version

Impact:

- Lower investment
- Faster validation

---

## Alternative 3: Buy Solution

Impact:

- Faster execution
- Vendor dependency

---

# 10. Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low adoption | High | Customer validation |
| Technical complexity | Medium | Prototype first |
| Cost overrun | Medium | Phased delivery |

---

# 11. Rollout Plan

## Phase 1: Validation

Goals:

- Test demand
- Validate assumptions

---

## Phase 2: MVP

Goals:

- Launch core functionality
- Measure adoption

---

## Phase 3: Scale

Goals:

- Expand capabilities
- Optimize economics

---

# 12. Final Recommendation

Use:

```
Initiative:

Problem:

Solution:

Investment:

Expected Impact:

ROI:

Risks:

Decision:
```

# Key Takeaways

A strong business case transforms:

```
Idea

↓

Opportunity

↓

Investment Decision
```

Great Product Managers do not only identify opportunities.

They build the argument for why those opportunities deserve investment.
