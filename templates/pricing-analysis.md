# Pricing Analysis Template

A practical framework for Product Managers to evaluate pricing decisions, packaging changes, and monetization opportunities.

Pricing is one of the highest-leverage product decisions because small improvements can significantly impact:

- Revenue
- Growth
- Customer value
- Profitability
- Market positioning

---

# Pricing Decision Framework

```
Customer Value

↓

Pricing Strategy

↓

Customer Behavior

↓

Revenue Impact

↓

Business Outcome
```

---

# 1. Pricing Overview

## Initiative Name

Example:

```
Introduce Enterprise Pricing Tier
```

---

## Business Goal

Define the objective.

Examples:

- Increase revenue
- Improve monetization
- Increase expansion revenue
- Reduce customer friction
- Improve market positioning

---

## Current Pricing Model

Document:

- Current plans
- Pricing levels
- Features included
- Customer segments

Example:

```
Free

↓

Professional

↓

Business
```

---

# 2. Customer Segmentation

Different customers have different willingness to pay.

Analyze:

## Segment

Examples:

- Individual users
- Small businesses
- Mid-market
- Enterprise

---

## Customer Characteristics

Consider:

- Company size
- Usage level
- Business value
- Budget
- Purchase behavior

---

# 3. Value Analysis

Pricing should reflect customer value.

Evaluate:

## Economic Value

Questions:

- How much money does the product create?
- How much cost does it remove?
- How much time does it save?

---

## Customer Outcomes

Examples:

Product creates:

- More revenue
- Higher productivity
- Lower operational costs
- Better customer experience

---

# Value Calculation

Example:

```
Time Saved:

10 hours/week

×

Employee Cost:

$50/hour

=

$500/week value
```

Create multiple alternatives.

- Churn risk

# Option B: New Tier

```

Evaluate:

- Target customers
- Features
- Sales impact

---

# Option C: Usage-Based Pricing

```

Evaluate:

- Customer alignment

# Option D: Packaging Change

```

```
Basic

+

+

# 5. Revenue Impact Model

Estimate impact.

---

## Customer Impact

Measure:

- Conversion rate
- Upgrade rate
- Downgrade rate
- Churn

---

## Revenue Formula

```
New Revenue

=

Customers

×

New Average Revenue

```

---

## Expansion Revenue

Formula:

```
Expansion ARR

=

Existing Customers

×

Upgrade Rate

×

Price Increase
```

## Conversion Rate

## Expansion Revenue
