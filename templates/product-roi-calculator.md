# Product ROI Calculator Template

A framework for Product Managers to evaluate whether a feature, initiative, or investment is worth building.

A strong product decision balances:

```
Customer Value

+

Business Impact

+

Investment Cost

+

Strategic Importance
```

---

# When To Use This Template

Use this model for:

- New feature proposals
- Platform investments
- Growth initiatives
- AI features
- Infrastructure improvements
- Product experiments
- Market expansion

---

# ROI Framework Overview

```
Investment

↓

Expected Impact

↓

Financial Value

↓

ROI Decision
```

---

# 1. Initiative Overview

## Initiative Name

Example:

```
AI Customer Support Assistant
```

---

## Problem Statement

Describe:

- Customer pain point
- Business challenge
- Current limitations

Example:

```
Customers spend too much time waiting for support responses.
```

---

## Proposed Solution

Explain:

- What will be built?
- Who will use it?
- How does it solve the problem?

---

# 2. Investment Calculation

## Development Cost

Include:

### Engineering

- Number of engineers
- Duration
- Estimated cost

Example:

```
4 engineers × 6 months
```

---

### Product & Design

Include:

- Product management
- UX research
- Design

---

### Infrastructure

Include:

- Cloud costs
- AI inference
- Storage
- Third-party services

---

### Operations

Include:

- Support
- Training
- Maintenance

---

# Total Investment

Formula:

```
Total Investment

=

Engineering Cost

+

Product Cost

+

Infrastructure Cost

+

Operational Cost
```

---

# 3. Business Impact

Evaluate possible outcomes.

---

# Revenue Impact

Potential sources:

- New customers
- Higher conversion
- Upsell
- Expansion revenue
- New pricing

Formula:

```
Incremental Revenue

=

Additional Customers

×

Average Revenue
```

---

# Retention Impact

Measure:

- Reduced churn
- Increased engagement
- Higher renewal rate

Example:

```
Churn improvement:

5%

↓

3%
```

---

# Cost Reduction

Examples:

- Automation
- Reduced support tickets
- Lower infrastructure cost
- Operational efficiency

Formula:

```
Annual Savings

=

Current Cost

-

New Cost
```

---

# 4. Financial Impact Model

## Total Value Created

Formula:

```
Total Value

=

Revenue Impact

+

Cost Savings

+

Retention Value
```

---

## ROI

Formula:

```
ROI

=

(Value Created - Investment)

/

Investment
```

---

## Payback Period

Formula:

```
Payback Period

=

Investment

/

Monthly Value Created
```

---

# 5. Scenario Analysis

Never evaluate only one outcome.

---

# Best Case

Assumptions:

- High adoption
- Strong revenue impact
- Lower costs

---

# Base Case

Most realistic scenario.

---

# Worst Case

Assumptions:

- Low adoption
- Higher costs
- Limited impact

---

# Example Scenario Table

| Scenario | Investment | Value Created | ROI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Best Case | $1M | $5M | 400% |
| Base Case | $1M | $2.5M | 150% |
| Worst Case | $1M | $0.8M | -20% |

---

# 6. Strategic Evaluation

Financial ROI is not the only factor.

Evaluate:

## Customer Importance

Questions:

- Does this solve a critical problem?
- Does it improve customer experience?

---

## Strategic Alignment

Questions:

- Does this support company strategy?
- Does it create competitive advantage?

---

## Market Impact

Questions:

- Does it open new markets?
- Does it improve positioning?

---

# 7. Decision Framework

Final recommendation:

## Invest

Use when:

- Strong ROI
- Strategic importance
- Clear customer value

---

## Experiment First

Use when:

- High uncertainty
- Need validation

---

## Do Not Invest

Use when:

- Low impact
- High opportunity cost

---

> "Can we build this?"

> "Should we invest in this?"
