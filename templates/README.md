# Templates

A collection of practical templates to help Product Managers apply FP&A, finance, and business strategy concepts.

These templates are designed for real-world product work:

- Evaluating investments
- Building business cases
- Forecasting outcomes
- Understanding product economics
- Communicating with executives

---

# Available Templates

## Financial Models

Templates for understanding product financial impact.

Includes:

- SaaS Revenue Model
- Product P&L
- Forecast Model
- Scenario Analysis

---

## Business Cases

Templates for proposing new initiatives.

Includes:

- Problem Definition
- Market Opportunity
- Investment Required
- Expected Impact
- ROI Analysis

---

## Pricing & Monetization

Templates for pricing decisions.

Includes:

- Pricing Analysis
- Packaging Evaluation
- Value-Based Pricing
- Revenue Impact Model

---

## Product Strategy

Templates for strategic decisions.

Includes:

- Product Portfolio Review
- Investment Memo
- Roadmap Prioritization
- Opportunity Assessment

---

## Executive Communication

Templates for leadership discussions.

Includes:

- Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
- Product Review
- Investment Proposal
- Strategy Document

---

# How To Use

1. Copy the template
2. Customize assumptions and inputs
3. Add product-specific data
4. Validate with stakeholders
5. Use insights for decision-making
