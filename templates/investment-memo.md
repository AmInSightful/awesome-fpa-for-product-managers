# Investment Memo Template

A strategic document for Product Managers to communicate investment opportunities, trade-offs, and expected business impact.

An investment memo helps answer:

- Why should we invest?
- Why now?
- How much should we invest?
- What return should we expect?
- What alternatives exist?

---

# Investment Decision Framework

```
Strategic Opportunity

↓

Investment Required

↓

Expected Business Impact

↓

Risk Assessment

↓

Recommendation
```

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Keep this section short.

Include:

- Investment proposal
- Strategic importance
- Expected impact
- Recommendation

Example:

```
Invest in AI-powered customer support automation
to reduce support costs by 30% and improve
customer response times.
```

---

# 2. Business Context

Explain the current situation.

Include:

- Market conditions
- Customer needs
- Business challenges
- Competitive environment

Questions:

- Why does this matter?
- What problem are we solving?
- Why now?

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Define the opportunity.

Structure:

```
Current Situation:

Problem:

Business Impact:

Customer Impact:
```

Example:

```
Enterprise customers lack advanced analytics.

This limits expansion opportunities and creates
competitive risk.
```

---

# 4. Strategic Alignment

Explain how the investment supports company strategy.

Evaluate:

## Company Goals

Examples:

- Revenue growth
- Market expansion
- Cost efficiency
- Customer retention

---

## Product Strategy

Explain:

- Which customer problem is addressed?
- Which product objective is supported?

---

## Competitive Advantage

Evaluate:

- Differentiation
- Market position
- Long-term advantage

---

# 5. Proposed Investment

Describe what will be built or changed.

Include:

## Scope

- Product capabilities
- Technical requirements
- Teams involved

---

## Timeline

Example:

```
Phase 1:
Discovery & Validation

Phase 2:
MVP Development

Phase 3:
Scale
```

---

# 6. Investment Requirements

Estimate resources.

---

## People

Include:

- Product managers
- Engineers
- Designers
- Data teams
- Operations

---

## Technology

Include:

- Infrastructure
- Software
- APIs
- AI models

---

## Budget

Include:

- Development cost
- Operating cost
- Marketing cost

---

# Total Investment

Formula:

```
Total Investment

=

People Cost

+

Technology Cost

+

Operational Cost
```

---

# 7. Expected Business Impact

Measure outcomes.

---

# Revenue Impact

Examples:

- New customers
- Upsell
- Expansion revenue
- Pricing opportunity

---

# Cost Impact

Examples:

- Automation savings
- Reduced support costs
- Infrastructure efficiency

---

# Customer Impact

Examples:

- Better retention
- Higher satisfaction
- Increased adoption

---

# 8. Financial Analysis

Include:

## ROI

Formula:

```
ROI

=

(Value Created - Investment)

/

Investment
```

---

## Payback Period

Formula:

```
Payback Period

=

Investment

/

Monthly Benefit
```

---

## Key Financial Metrics

Examples:

- ARR impact
- Gross margin impact
- Contribution margin
- CAC impact
- LTV impact

---

# 9. Alternatives Considered

Good investment decisions consider alternatives.

---

## Option A

Build internally

Pros:

- More control
- Custom solution

Cons:

- Higher investment
- Longer timeline

---

## Option B

Buy Existing Solution

Pros:

- Faster execution

Cons:

- Vendor dependency

---

## Option C

Do Nothing

Evaluate:

- Opportunity cost
- Competitive risk
- Customer impact

---

# 10. Risk Assessment

Identify risks.

---

## Market Risk

Questions:

- Will customers adopt?
- Is demand validated?

---

## Technical Risk

Questions:

- Can we build it?
- Are dependencies clear?

---

## Financial Risk

Questions:

- Are assumptions realistic?
- Is ROI uncertain?

---

## Execution Risk

Questions:

- Do we have resources?
- Is timeline realistic?

---

# 11. Success Metrics

Define measurement criteria.

---

## Business Metrics

Examples:

- Revenue
- ARR
- Margin
- Cost savings

---

## Product Metrics

Examples:

- Adoption
- Engagement
- Retention
- Activation

---

## Customer Metrics

Examples:

- Satisfaction
- NPS
- Support reduction

---

# 12. Final Recommendation

Use:

```
Recommendation:

Investment Amount:

Expected Return:

Timeline:

Key Risks:

Success Criteria:
```

Before presenting:

☐ Investment estimated

☐ Alternatives evaluated

☐ Risks documented

☐ Success metrics defined

# Key Takeaways

An investment memo transforms:

```
Product Idea

↓

Business Case

↓

Strategic Decision
```

The strongest Product Managers do not only request resources.

They explain why investment creates value.
