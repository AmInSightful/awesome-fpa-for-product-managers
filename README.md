# Awesome FP&A for Product Managers [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated collection of the best resources on FP&A, strategic finance, pricing, forecasting, financial modeling, SaaS metrics, AI, and business decision-making for Product Managers.

Product Managers are increasingly expected to make financially informed decisions—not just build features. Whether you're prioritizing roadmap investments, evaluating pricing strategies, forecasting growth, or communicating with executives, understanding FP&A and strategic finance is becoming a core product management skill.

This repository brings together the best books, articles, newsletters, courses, podcasts, templates, tools, and case studies to help Product Managers think like business owners.

Contributions are welcome! Please read the contribution guidelines before opening a Pull Request.
