# AI for FP&A

Artificial Intelligence is transforming FP&A from a reporting function into a strategic decision-making capability.

Traditional FP&A focuses on:

- Budgeting
- Forecasting
- Reporting
- Variance analysis

Modern AI-powered FP&A adds:

- Automated analysis
- Predictive forecasting
- Intelligent recommendations
- Scenario simulation
- Executive decision support

For Product Managers, AI for FP&A creates a bridge between:

```
Product Data

↓

Business Intelligence

↓

Financial Analysis

↓

Strategic Decisions
```

---

# Why PMs Should Care About AI for FP&A

Product teams generate enormous amounts of data:

- User behavior
- Revenue metrics
- Customer feedback
- Usage patterns
- Experiment results

AI helps transform this data into business insights.

Examples:

- Predict feature adoption
- Forecast revenue impact
- Explain metric changes
- Identify growth opportunities
- Automate business reviews

# AI Forecasting

AI models can improve forecasting by analyzing:

- Historical revenue
- Customer behavior
- Seasonality
- Market signals
- Product usage

Example:

Traditional:

```
Revenue grew 20% last quarter

↓

Assume 20% growth
```

AI-powered:

```
Revenue

+

Customer cohorts

+

Usage trends

+

Market signals

↓

Forecast
```

---

# AI Variance Analysis

Variance analysis compares:

```
Actual Performance

vs

Plan / Forecast
```

Traditional process:

Analyst manually investigates differences.

AI approach:

```
Revenue missed target by 8%

↓

AI identifies drivers:

- Lower enterprise conversion
- Higher churn
- Pricing impact
```
