# Portfolio Strategy

Portfolio Strategy is the discipline of managing investments across multiple products, markets, customers, and initiatives to maximize business value.

For Product Managers, portfolio strategy answers:

> "Where should we invest our limited resources to create the highest strategic and financial impact?"

A company rarely has unlimited:

- Engineering capacity
- Budget
- Time
- Talent

Portfolio strategy helps decide where to focus.

---

# Why PMs Should Care

Product Managers often make decisions at the feature level.

Senior Product Leaders make decisions at the portfolio level.

Portfolio thinking helps answer:

- Which products should grow?
- Which products should be maintained?
- Which products should be retired?
- Where should we invest more?
- Which opportunities should we ignore?

---

# Product Portfolio Thinking

A company portfolio usually contains:

```
Growth Products

+

Core Products

+

Emerging Bets

+

Legacy Products
```

Each category requires a different strategy.

---

# Product Portfolio Categories

---

# Core Products

Products that generate current revenue and customer value.

Focus:

- Improve reliability
- Increase retention
- Expand adoption
- Protect market position

Examples:

- Mature SaaS products
- Main revenue products

---

# Growth Products

Products with strong growth potential.

Focus:

- Increase adoption
- Scale distribution
- Improve monetization

Key metrics:

- Growth rate
- Customer adoption
- Revenue expansion

---

# Emerging Bets

Early products with uncertain outcomes.

Focus:

- Validate assumptions
- Test market demand
- Learn quickly

Examples:

- New AI products
- New markets
- Experimental products

---

# Legacy Products

Products that still generate value but may have limited future growth.

Focus:

- Reduce costs
- Maintain customers
- Avoid unnecessary investment

---

# Product Investment Framework

Before investing:

## 1. Strategic Alignment

Questions:

- Does this support company strategy?
- Does it strengthen our position?
- Does it create differentiation?

---

## 2. Market Opportunity

Evaluate:

- Market size
- Growth rate
- Competition
- Customer demand

---

## 3. Financial Impact

Consider:

- Revenue potential
- Cost savings
- Margin improvement
- ROI

---

## 4. Execution Complexity

Evaluate:

- Engineering effort
- Dependencies
- Timeline
- Risks

# Portfolio Management Frameworks

---

# BCG Matrix

One of the most famous portfolio frameworks.

Axes:

```
Market Growth

+

Market Share
```

Categories:

## Stars

High growth, high market share.

Strategy:

Invest aggressively.

---

## Cash Cows

Low growth, high market share.

Strategy:

Generate profit and fund investments.

---

## Question Marks

High growth, low market share.

Strategy:

Decide whether to invest or exit.

---

## Dogs

Low growth, low market share.

Strategy:

Reduce investment or retire.

---

# GE / McKinsey Matrix

Evaluates products based on:

```
Market Attractiveness

+

Competitive Strength
```

Useful for larger portfolios.

---

# RICE Framework

A practical product prioritization framework.

Formula:

```
Reach

×

Impact

×

Confidence

/

Effort
```

Used for:

- Feature prioritization
- Roadmap decisions

---

# Opportunity Cost

Portfolio strategy is fundamentally about trade-offs.

Choosing:

```
Investment A
```

means not choosing:

```
Investment B
```

The cost is the value of the alternative opportunity.

---

# Build vs Buy Decisions

Companies frequently decide:

Should we build internally?

or

Should we buy?

Evaluate:

## Build

Advantages:

- More control
- Customization
- Strategic ownership

Costs:

- Time
- Engineering resources
- Maintenance

---

## Buy

Advantages:

- Faster execution
- Existing expertise

Costs:

- Vendor dependency
- Integration complexity

```
Introduction

Growth

↓

Maturity

Decline
```

Investment strategy changes by stage.

# Growth Stage

- Distribution
- Monetization

Metrics:

- Revenue growth
- Adoption
- Retention

---

# Maturity Stage

Focus:

- Efficiency
- Expansion
- Optimization

Metrics:

- NRR
