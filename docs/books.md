# Books

A curated reading list for Product Managers who want to develop strong financial, strategic, and business decision-making skills.

The goal is not to turn PMs into accountants.

The goal is to help PMs think like business owners.

---

# Product Management

Books about building products, discovering customer needs, and creating value.

---

## Inspired

**Author:** Marty Cagan

Why read:

A foundational product management book covering:

- Product discovery
- Product teams
- Empowered decision-making
- Building valuable products

Key takeaway:

Great products come from understanding customer problems, not just executing requirements.

---

## Empowered

**Author:** Marty Cagan

Why read:

Explains how high-performing product organizations operate.

Covers:

- Product leadership
- Product teams
- Strategy
- Autonomy

Key takeaway:

Strong product teams are empowered to solve problems, not just deliver features.

---

## Escaping the Build Trap

**Author:** Melissa Perri

Why read:

Explains the shift from output-focused teams to outcome-focused organizations.

Covers:

- Product strategy
- Outcomes
- Product operating models

Key takeaway:

Shipping more features does not necessarily create more value.

---

## Continuous Discovery Habits

**Author:** Teresa Torres

Why read:

A practical framework for continuous customer discovery.

Covers:

- Customer interviews
- Opportunity mapping
- Product experiments

Key takeaway:

Great products are built through continuous learning.

---

# Finance Fundamentals

Books that help PMs understand business and financial decisions.

---

## Financial Intelligence

**Authors:** Karen Berman & Joe Knight

Why read:

One of the best introductions to finance for non-finance professionals.

Covers:

- Income statements
- Balance sheets
- Cash flow
- Profitability

Key takeaway:

Every business decision has a financial impact.

---

## The Personal MBA

**Author:** Josh Kaufman

Why read:

A broad overview of business fundamentals.

Covers:

- Value creation
- Marketing
- Sales
- Finance
- Operations

Key takeaway:

Understanding business systems improves decision-making.

---

## Valuation

**Authors:** McKinsey & Company

Why read:

A deep guide to understanding company value.

Covers:

- Cash flows
- Growth
- Returns
- Business valuation

Key takeaway:

Company value comes from sustainable cash generation.

---

## Financial Modeling

**Author:** Simon Benninga

Why read:

A practical guide to building financial models.

Covers:

- Forecasting
- Scenario analysis
- Valuation models

Key takeaway:

Models help leaders make better decisions under uncertainty.

---

# Strategy

Books about strategic thinking and competitive advantage.

---

## Good Strategy Bad Strategy

**Author:** Richard Rumelt

Why read:

Explains what real strategy looks like.

Covers:

- Strategic diagnosis
- Focus
- Competitive advantage

Key takeaway:

Strategy is about making choices, not creating vague goals.

---

## Playing to Win

**Authors:** A.G. Lafley & Roger Martin

Why read:

A practical strategy framework.

Covers:

- Where to play
- How to win
- Strategic choices

Key takeaway:

Winning requires clear choices.

---

## Blue Ocean Strategy

**Authors:** W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne

Why read:

Explains how companies create new market spaces.

Covers:

- Differentiation
- Value innovation
- Market creation

Key takeaway:

The best opportunities often come from redefining competition.

---

## 7 Powers

**Author:** Hamilton Helmer

Why read:

Explains sources of durable competitive advantage.

Covers:

- Scale economies
- Network effects
- Switching costs
- Brand

Key takeaway:

Great companies build structural advantages.

---

# SaaS & Subscription Business

Books about recurring revenue businesses.

---

## Subscribed

**Author:** Tien Tzuo

Why read:

Explains subscription business models.

Covers:

- Subscription economics
- Customer relationships
- Recurring revenue

Key takeaway:

Modern businesses optimize customer lifetime value, not one-time sales.

---

## The SaaS Metrics Blueprint

Why read:

Useful introduction to SaaS measurement.

Covers:

- ARR
- MRR
- CAC
- LTV
- Retention

Key takeaway:

SaaS success depends on efficient growth.

# Pricing & Monetization

Books about capturing value.

---

## Monetizing Innovation

**Authors:** Madhavan Ramanujam & Georg Tacke

Why read:

A practical guide to pricing innovation.

Covers:

- Customer value
- Pricing research
- Monetization strategy

Key takeaway:

Pricing should be designed before building the product.

---

## Confessions of the Pricing Man

**Author:** Hermann Simon

Why read:

A global perspective on pricing strategy.

Covers:

- Value pricing
- Price psychology
- Monetization

Key takeaway:

Pricing is one of the biggest business growth levers.

---

## The Strategy and Tactics of Pricing

**Authors:** Thomas Nagle, Georg Müller

Why read:

A detailed pricing framework.

Covers:

- Segmentation
- Value measurement
- Pricing execution

---

- Uncertainty
- Evidence-based thinking

Better decisions come from better thinking processes.

## Thinking in Bets

**Author:** Annie Duke

Why read:

Explains decision-making under uncertainty.

Covers:

- Risk
- Outcomes vs decisions
