# Dashboards & BI

Business Intelligence (BI) is the practice of transforming raw data into insights that support better decisions.

For Product Managers and FP&A professionals, dashboards are not just reporting tools.

They are decision systems.

A good dashboard connects:

```
Business Goals

↓

Key Metrics

↓

Product Behavior

↓

Actions
```

---

# Why PMs Should Care About Dashboards

Senior Product Managers need visibility into:

- Product performance
- Customer behavior
- Revenue impact
- Operational health
- Strategic goals

Dashboards help answer:

- Are we winning?
- Why are metrics changing?
- What needs attention?
- Where should we invest?

---

# Dashboard Principles

A good dashboard should be:

## Actionable

Every metric should support a decision.

Bad:

```
Number of users
```

Better:

```
Activation dropped 15% after onboarding change
```

---

## Simple

Avoid unnecessary metrics.

Focus on:

- Important trends
- Key drivers
- Business outcomes

---

## Connected to Goals

Metrics should map to:

```
Company Strategy

↓

Product Strategy

↓

Team Objectives

↓

Metrics
```

# Metrics Layer

A mature analytics organization creates a shared definition of metrics.

Example:

Instead of different teams calculating ARR differently:

```
One ARR Definition

↓

All Dashboards Use It
```

Benefits:

- Trust
- Consistency
- Faster decisions

# Dashboard Design Framework

## Step 1: Define Decision

Ask:

"What decision should this dashboard support?"

---

## Step 2: Identify Metrics

Choose:

- Outcome metrics
- Driver metrics
- Diagnostic metrics

---

## Step 3: Add Context

Include:

- Targets
- Historical trends
- Benchmarks

---

## Step 4: Create Actions

Every dashboard should answer:

"What should we do next?"
