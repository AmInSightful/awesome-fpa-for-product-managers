# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative community around **Awesome FP&A for Product Managers**.

This repository exists to help Product Managers learn about:

- Finance
- Strategic planning
- Business decision-making
- Product economics
- Data-driven product management

Everyone should feel comfortable contributing and sharing knowledge.

---

# Expected Behavior

Community members should:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Share knowledge openly
- Provide helpful feedback
- Focus on improving the resource quality
- Respect different backgrounds and experiences

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Insults or disrespectful communication
- Spam or excessive self-promotion
- Deliberately misleading information
- Any behavior that discourages participation

---

# Contribution Standards

When contributing:

- Add resources that provide real value
- Explain why a resource is useful
- Respect existing organization and structure
- Accept feedback from maintainers

The goal is quality, not quantity.

---

# Handling Issues

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior:

- Open a private discussion with repository maintainers
- Provide relevant details
- Allow maintainers to review the situation

---

# Community Principle

We believe:

```
Better Knowledge

+

Better Decisions

+

Better Products
```

A strong community helps everyone become a better Product Manager.

---

Thank you for helping build a valuable learning resource.
